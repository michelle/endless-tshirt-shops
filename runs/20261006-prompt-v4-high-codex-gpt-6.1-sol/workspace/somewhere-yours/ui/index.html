A few field notes

Good things
to know.

Is the landscape a map of my location?

It’s an original abstract landscape inspired by your coordinates, place name and date. The coordinates are printed exactly as you enter them, but the contours are artistic and don’t represent actual terrain. Check your location in your map app before approving.

What shirt will I receive?

A Gildan 64000 Softstyle unisex tee, with your customized design printed on the front using direct-to-garment technology. Black, natural and white are available in S–2XL. These solid colors are cotton. The digital shirt illustration is a preview; order a physical sample before relying on exact placement or colors.

How long does delivery take?

The store currently runs in sandbox mode, so test orders aren’t physically printed or shipped. For live orders, production and transit vary by facility and destination. Shipping estimates must be confirmed before the production launch. This store currently accepts US addresses only.

Can I change or return a personalized shirt?

Check every detail before paying: each shirt is made to your approved design. Production can start quickly after payment. For damaged, incorrect or defective items, save your order reference and photos. The merchant must publish a support address and final returns policy before accepting live orders.

How do I care for a DTG print?

Wash inside out with like colors on a cool, gentle cycle. Use mild detergent; avoid bleach and ironing directly over the print. Air drying helps preserve the artwork.
