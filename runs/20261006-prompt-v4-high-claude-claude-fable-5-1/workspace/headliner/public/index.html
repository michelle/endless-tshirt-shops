How it works

  1. You write the setlist. Tour name, a kicker, and the dates. The preview is the actual print file, not a mockup.
  2. We typeset it like a tour tee. Chest logo on the front, full dates on the back, with the type sized to fit however many stops you have.
  3. Printed to order, one of one. Direct-to-garment printing means your shirt is printed the moment you pay. No minimums, no blanks sitting in a warehouse.

Good questions

What if my dates are embarrassing?

That's the point. The best tour shirts have at least one stop nobody wants to talk about.

Can I put a dog on tour?

Yes. Dogs, bands, book clubs, the 2019 fantasy league. Anything with a history.

Will the print hold up?

DTG ink is water-based and soaks into the cotton. Wash inside out, cold, and it ages like a real tour shirt should.
