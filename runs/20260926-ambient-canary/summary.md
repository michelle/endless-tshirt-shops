# VESPERGLASS-R7Q4 — personalized tidal fingerprint shirts

This archived storefront turns a customer's chosen coastline, date, and tide
height into a one-of-one DTG print. The design uses nested shoreline contours
and a copper registration mark, with the customer's short dedication typeset
underneath. Its internal concept label is `tidal-fingerprint-vestige`.

The storefront was deployed as a short-run experiment and is retained here with
its original name and concept description.
