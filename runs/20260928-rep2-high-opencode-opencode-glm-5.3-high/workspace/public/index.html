    How a Nightshift shirt is made

    1. Your moment and place give one exact instant — stars, constellations and the Moon are computed for it, in your timezone.
    2. The sky is drawn once, full size, and you see the very file that will print.
    3. After payment, the order goes to a print lab near the recipient and ships directly to you. No two are ever the same.

    Nightshift — a demo store. Sky computed from the HYG star catalogue with the Moon placed by a short lunar theory; printed via the Prodigi Print API; paid for via Stripe. Test data, no real charges or shipments.
