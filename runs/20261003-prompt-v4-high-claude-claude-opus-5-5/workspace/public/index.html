Questions

How is it printed?

Direct-to-garment (DTG): the ink goes into the cotton, so there's no stiff vinyl and no minimum order. Each shirt is printed after you order. Station centres are left unprinted on light tees and printed white on dark tees, so they stay crisp either way.

Which shirt is it?

The Bella+Canvas 3001: soft, 100% combed ring-spun cotton (heathers are blends), unisex retail fit. If you're between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit.

How long does it take?

Printing takes 2–4 business days. Standard tracked shipping usually adds 5–10 business days, Express 2–5. You'll get a link to follow your order.

Can I order for a group?

Yes. Add as many sizes as you need (up to 20 shirts per order). They all get the same design.

What if something's wrong?

If a shirt arrives misprinted or damaged, get in touch with a photo within 30 days and we'll reprint it for free. Typos you made yourself aren't covered, so read every stop carefully before you pay.
