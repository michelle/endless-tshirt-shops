Why Direct-to-Garment (DTG) Changes Everything

DTG technology enables genuine one-of-a-kind astronomical apparel with zero waste.

✦

100% Unique Every Order

Traditional screen printing requires hundreds of identical shirts. DTG injects micro-pigment water-based ink directly into the ring-spun cotton fibers, making individual celestial charts feasible.

◎

Astrophysical Precision

We compute Julian Day, Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time (GMST), and local altitude/azimuth across 80+ Yale Bright Star coordinates with stereographic celestial projection.

✧

Bella + Canvas 3001 Quality

Printed onto retail-quality 100% Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton. Pre-shrunk, super-soft hand feel with seamless durability across washes.
