Good to know

How long until it arrives?

Shirts are made to order. Production typically takes 2–5 business days, then standard shipping (usually 3–10 business days depending on where you are). You'll get a tracking link on your order page as soon as it ships.

How accurate is the sky?

Planet positions are calculated from NASA/JPL approximate orbital elements and are within about half a degree of the precise ephemeris, far finer than the print can show. The view is looking down on the solar system from above the north pole, with the degree ring showing ecliptic longitude. Positions are computed for noon UTC on your date. Orbit spacing is illustrative, not to scale.

What's the shirt like?

Unisex Gildan Softstyle 64000, 100% ring-spun cotton, a soft, classic fit. If you're between sizes, size up. Chest widths: S 18″, M 20″, L 22″, XL 24″, 2XL 26″.

Will the print last?

It's printed directly into the fibres with water-based inks (DTG). Wash inside out in cool water and tumble dry low or line dry for best results.

Can I return it?

Because every shirt is made specially for you, we can't accept returns for change of mind or wrong size. If something arrives damaged, misprinted or incorrect, contact us within 30 days with a photo and we'll reprint or refund it.

Which countries do you ship to?

Over 100 countries, with the exact list shown at checkout. Shipping is quoted live for your destination. Import duties or local taxes may apply on delivery outside your production region.
