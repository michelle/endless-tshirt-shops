How the dial reads

The river of light. Around the year, day by day: how long the sun stayed up. Wide in summer, pinched in winter — at true solar time, so noon is always the middle of the band.

Graded twilight. Civil, nautical and astronomical dusk, in three blues. Where they vanish, the sun never set: the midnight sun.

The starfield. Hours of true night, seeded from your coordinates — the same sky, same stars, every time you order.
