Charting…

How your sky becomes a shirt

Not a template. A computation.

I · Chart

We take your date, time and coordinates and solve the real sky: 5,000 catalogue stars, the 88 constellation figures, the Milky Way's contours, the Moon in its true phase and orientation, and any planets above your horizon — projected exactly as they stood overhead.

II · Engrave

Your moment is set in type beneath the chart: your words, your date, your place, your coordinates. The artwork is rendered as a 4,000-pixel print master with a transparent background, so the shirt's own fabric becomes the night.

III · Print & ship

After payment clears, the master goes straight to our print partner's direct-to-garment presses — printed, packed and shipped to your door. One shirt, one sky, one moment.
