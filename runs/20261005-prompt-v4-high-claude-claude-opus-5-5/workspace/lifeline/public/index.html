    Questions

    What shirt is it?

    The Bella+Canvas 3001: 100% combed ring-spun cotton (heathers are cotton/poly), side-seamed and retail fit. It’s the softest everyday tee we know. Sizes XS–4XL, unisex.

    How is it printed?

    Direct-to-garment (DTG): the ink goes straight into the fabric, like a photo printer for shirts. That’s why every shirt can be different, with no setup fees. On dark shirts, a white underbase keeps colours bright.

    Can I return it?

    Every shirt is made just for you, so we can’t take back changes of heart. If anything arrives misprinted or damaged, we’ll reprint it free.

    Will my text fit?

    The preview is the real print file. Long names shrink a little to fit. If it looks good here, it’ll look good on the shirt.
