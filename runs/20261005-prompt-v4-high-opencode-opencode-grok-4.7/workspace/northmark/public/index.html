How a plate is made

You name a place, a date, and an hour. Northmark computes the sky that was actually overhead — horizon at the ring, north at the top — and sets your title and dedication in the same plate. The file is a transparent PNG at the garment’s print size, so only the ink lands on the cloth.

The shirt

Gildan 2000, a 6 oz classic unisex tee, printed direct-to-garment. Dark shirts take a cream and gold plate. Light shirts take ink and rust. One front print, composed to sit on the chest rather than bleed to the hem.

After you pay

Stripe confirms the charge. Only then does Northmark hand the plate and your address to Prodigi, who print and ship from the lab nearest you. In this proof studio the handoff is real, the printing is not.
