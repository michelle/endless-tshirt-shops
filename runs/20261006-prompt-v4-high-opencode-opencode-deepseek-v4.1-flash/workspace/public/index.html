FAQ

Good to know.

What exactly is a soundprint?

It's a waveform — the visual shape of sound — composed deterministically from your phrase. The same words always produce the same shape; different words produce a completely different one.

How is it printed?

Direct-to-garment (DTG). Your design is printed with water-based inks directly into the fabric, so it's soft to the touch and handles fine detail and gradients beautifully.

What shirt is it?

A unisex Bella+Canvas 3001 — a soft, medium-weight 100% cotton tee. Sizes S–3XL. Colour is your choice.

How long does it take?

Production typically takes 2–5 business days, then shipping. You'll receive tracking by email.

Can I return it?

Because every shirt is made for one person, made-to-order items can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or misprinted. If something's wrong, contact us and we'll reprint it.
