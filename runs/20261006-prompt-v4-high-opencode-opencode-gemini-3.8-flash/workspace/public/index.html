The Manufacturing Advantage

Why Direct-to-Garment (DTG) Changes Everything

✦

True 1-of-1 Personalization

Screen printing requires cutting separate mesh screens for each color and design, costing $50+ in setup fees per order. DTG prints directly from 300 DPI vector digital rasters, allowing us to compute and render a unique star chart for every customer at zero extra cost.

◎

Full Dynamic Range & Star Magnitude

Our print files are rendered at 4,680 × 5,790 pixels with anti-aliased transparency. From 1st-magnitude beacons like Sirius to 4th-magnitude background nebulae, DTG faithfully recreates delicate star glows and fine coordinate rings.

☼

Water-Based Oeko-Tex® Inks

No stiff, rubbery plastic feeling. Our DTG printers use eco-friendly water-based inks that bind into the organic cotton fibers, leaving a breathable, feather-light hand feel that softens further with every wash.
