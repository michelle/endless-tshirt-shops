# Asset provenance

## Landscape prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high-resolution portrait 3:4 print artwork for a personalized t-shirt store. Primary request: original vintage woodcut national-park style landscape illustration. Sage green conifer forest in foreground, dark green layered mountains, a terracotta round sun in a golden ochre sky. Beautiful restrained woodcut shapes and subtle cream paper grain texture. Composition: flat rectangular full-bleed illustration occupying every edge of canvas, portrait 3:4. Constraints: NO text, NO lettering, NO border, NO t-shirt, NO mockup, NO frame. Illustration only.

## Shirt prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: portrait ecommerce product photography. Primary request: photorealistic premium editorial flatlay of a white cotton crewneck t-shirt on warm cream stone backdrop. Composition: camera directly overhead; entire shirt centered and fully visible including sleeves and hem, straight aligned upright, collar at top. Broad smooth blank chest area for adding a print later. Natural garment proportions. Portrait composition. Lighting: soft natural daylight with subtle natural shadow. Materials: high quality white cotton with realistic soft folds and fine texture, warm cream matte stone surface. Constraints: completely BLANK shirt front, no text, no logo, no artwork, no tags visible, no props, no person.
