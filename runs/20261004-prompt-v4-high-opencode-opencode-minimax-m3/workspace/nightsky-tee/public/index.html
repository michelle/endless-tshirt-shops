About the printing

Direct-to-garment (DTG) printing lays water-based ink directly into the cotton fibres of a soft combed-ring-spun tee, so the design becomes part of the fabric — not a layer of plastic on top. Vibrant colours, soft hand-feel, no peeling or cracking, and the fabric still breathes.

We use Prodigi’s global print network, which routes every order to the lab nearest the recipient. A shirt sold in Tokyo is printed in Tokyo. A shirt sold in Berlin is printed in Europe. Lower carbon, faster delivery, and identical print quality everywhere.

The design data is computed deterministically from your input — the same time, the same place, will always produce the same shirt. Astro catalog source: Yale Bright Star Catalog.
