HOW IT WORKS

From moment to garment

01

Choose your moment

Pick the date, time and place that matters — down to the minute. We resolve the exact timezone (including daylight saving) for that place and date.

02

We compute your sky

Real astronomy, not clip art: 25,000+ stars from ESA's Hipparcos catalog, IAU constellation figures, the moon's true phase and orientation, and the positions of Mercury through Saturn — projected for your horizon.

03

Printed & shipped

After your payment clears, your artwork is rendered at 300 dpi and sent to our printing partner. Direct-to-garment inks reproduce every faint star and gradient. Printed in the lab closest to you, shipped to your door.

THE GARMENT

Premium in every thread

Blank

Bella+Canvas 3001 — 100% combed, ring-spun cotton, 4.2 oz. Retail fit, unisex. Sideseamed. Honestly one of the softest shirts you'll own.

Print

Direct-to-garment (DTG) with water-based inks. Your sky is printed as a ~10.6″ medallion on the chest with soft, breathable detail — gradients and thousands of tiny stars that screen printing simply can't do.

Palette guide

Ivory Negative is printed with the fabric — the shirt itself becomes your sky. Choose it on white, cream or grey. Midnight, Obsidian and Dusk are self-contained skies that shine on any color.

Care

Machine wash cold, inside-out, with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang. No bleach, no ironing the print. Your sky will outlast countless washes.

QUESTIONS

Good ones

Is the sky really accurate?

Yes. Star positions come from ESA's Hipparcos catalog (accurate to arcseconds), and the moon, sun and planets are computed with professional-grade ephemerides for your exact minute, timezone and coordinates. The moon is drawn at its true phase, with the lit side facing the real sun.

What if my moment happened during the day?

Your stars are still drawn exactly where they were — you just couldn't see them with the naked eye. We'll add a small golden sun to the map so the moment reads honestly. Many birthday shirts are midday skies.

Why direct-to-garment printing?

Because your sky has ~25,000 stars, delicate gradients and a true-phase moon. DTG prints full-color detail straight onto the fabric — the only technology that can carry this design faithfully. It's also zero-inventory: your shirt only exists after you order it.

How long does delivery take?

Production is 2–5 business days; shipping to the US and Europe typically 3–7 business days after that. Your order page shows live status from the print lab, including tracking once dispatched.

Can I return it?

Because every shirt is made just for you, we can't accept change-of-mind returns — but if your shirt arrives damaged or misprinted, we'll reprint or refund it immediately. Double-check your moment and spelling before ordering; the preview is exactly what prints.

Where does my shirt ship from?

Our print network produces your tee in the facility closest to your delivery address (US, EU, UK, AU and more), which keeps delivery fast and emissions lower.
