SOUVENIRS OF A LIFE WELL LIVED

stay with you.

We make tees for those places. The quiet weekend, the wrong turn that became the right one, the trip you never wanted to end.

Choose a place, a moment, and a landscape that feels like your memory. We turn them into an original poster illustration, ready to become your favorite shirt.

See you out there. ↗

A few good things to know.

What’s the shirt like?

Gildan 64000 Softstyle, with a unisex semi-fitted cut, crew neck, and lightweight ring-spun cotton. Black and white are 100% cotton. One full-color front print.

How does personalization work?

Your place, date, and words change the type and a seeded illustration. Choose desert, coast, or alpine scenery, then a color palette. The illustration is an interpretation of your memory, rather than a map or photograph.

Where do you ship?

We currently offer US delivery. Shipping is quoted by Prodigi for your shirt and shown before checkout. In sandbox mode, orders are test orders and no physical shirt is shipped.

How should I care for it?

Wash inside out on a cold, gentle cycle. Air dry when possible. Avoid bleach and ironing directly on the print.

Corrections, returns & privacy

Check your preview carefully before paying. Custom products cannot be changed once production starts. For damage or print defects, retain photos and your order number. This sandbox does not yet have a staffed support channel or a finalized return policy. Your delivery details are shared with Stripe and Prodigi for payment and fulfillment; they are not printed on your tee.
