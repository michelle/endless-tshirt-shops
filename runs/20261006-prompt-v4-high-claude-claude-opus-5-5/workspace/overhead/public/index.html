How it works

01

Choose your moment

Any place on Earth, any minute from 1900 to 2099. We convert your local time, time zone and all, into the exact sky.

02

We plot the heavens

Around 2,300 real stars from the Hipparcos and Yale Bright Star catalogues, 88 constellations, the planets and the Moon in its true phase, all computed for your coordinates.

03

Printed just for you

Your design is printed direct-to-garment on a premium cotton tee and shipped, tracked, to your door, usually within 5–12 business days.

The tee

Bella+Canvas 3001

The tee printers love: 100% combed and ring-spun cotton, a soft retail fit, side-seamed and unisex. Sizes XS–4XL depending on colour.

Built for DTG

The artwork is drawn as vectors and rendered at 300 DPI with solid, opaque inks. Lines and stars are never thinner than about 0.6 mm, so every detail actually prints.

Questions

How accurate is the sky?

Star positions come from professional catalogues and are rotated for the exact date, time and place. Planet and Moon positions use the open-source Astronomy Engine, accurate to well under a degree. The chart is drawn as if you were lying on your back looking up: north at the top, east on the left.

What if the moment was during the day?

We still show the stars that were above you. They were there, just hidden by sunlight. The preview tells you when the Sun was up.

Returns?

Every shirt is one of a kind, so we can't take returns for a change of mind. If anything arrives misprinted or damaged, we'll reprint it free.

Where do you ship?

The US, Canada, the UK, most of Europe, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong and Mexico. Shipping is calculated at cost from our print partner.
