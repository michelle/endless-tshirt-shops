Questions

Is the star map accurate?

Yes. We use the HYG/d3-celestial bright-star catalogue and standard sidereal-time maths to project the real sky for your date, time and coordinates. Constellation lines show the classic figures, and the Moon shows its true phase for that night.

What if I don't know the exact time?

Pick your best guess. A few hours shifts the map gently; the caption, names and date stay exactly as you set them.

How does the design look on a dark shirt?

We automatically switch the ink palette: a warm white-and-gold sky on dark shirts, and a deep navy-and-bronze sky on light shirts. Every design is printed on a transparent background.

When will it arrive?

Production takes 2–5 business days, then shipping. This is a made-to-order item, so returns are limited to printing faults — see our policies below.
