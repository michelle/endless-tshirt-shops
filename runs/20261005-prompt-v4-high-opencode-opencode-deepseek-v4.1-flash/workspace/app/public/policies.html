Policies & shipping

This is a test-mode demonstration store. The copy below describes how the store is intended to operate; it is not yet a live commercial offer.

Made to order

Every shirt is printed individually from the design you create. Because each item is produced on demand, we cannot accept returns for a change of mind. If your shirt arrives with a printing fault, a damaged item, or the wrong size or colour, contact support and we will reprint or refund it.

Production & shipping

Production takes 2–5 business days, after which your order ships. Shipping is handled by Prodigi's global lab network, which routes each order to the facility closest to the destination. Tracking is provided where the carrier supports it. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and customs duties may apply on international orders.

Payment

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see your card details. Your order is only sent to print after Stripe confirms that payment has succeeded. This demo runs in Stripe test mode and the Prodigi sandbox, so no real money is charged and no physical shirt is produced.

Artwork & personal data

The design is generated from the date, time, place and text you provide. We store only what is needed to fulfil the order, and the artwork URL is signed so it cannot be guessed. The star map is an astronomical projection for the coordinates and instant you choose; it is decorative and not a certified navigational chart.
