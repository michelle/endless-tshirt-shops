Questions

Where does the weather come from?

From the ERA5 reanalysis published by Copernicus / ECMWF, served through the Open-Meteo historical archive. It covers every point on Earth, hourly, since 1940. It is a model reconstruction blended with observations, so it reflects the day faithfully but may differ by a degree or two from a specific weather station.

Why can't I pick yesterday?

The archive is finalised a few days after the fact. We hold a one-week margin so your shirt is printed from settled data.

How do I read the design?

It is a 24-hour clock: midnight at the bottom, noon at the top. Each wedge is one hour. Its colour is that hour's sky (night, dawn, day, dusk, shaded by cloud and darkened by rain or lightened by snow). Its length is the temperature relative to the day's range. Dotted lines mark sunrise and sunset. Ticks outside the ring show rain; dots show snow. The moon shows its real phase that night.
