How a NOCTURNE is made

i.

THE SKY IS COMPUTED

We solve sidereal time for your exact minute, rotate 6,317 catalogue stars (Hipparcos/HYG) into your local horizon frame, project them azimuthally around your zenith, draw the Milky Way along its true galactic band and set the moon to its real phase.

ii.

THE CHART IS ENGRAVED

Your sky becomes a 4680×5790 px plate — the precise 300 dpi front print area of the shirt — with your title, coordinates and the moment set in type. Same inputs, always the identical plate.

iii.

ONE SHIRT, ONE RUN

After Stripe confirms payment, the plate goes to our print partner's direct-to-garment line and ships to your door. No stock, no reprints of your night — edition of one.
